Privacy and access

The right conversation should reach the right people—and no one else.

Lobby separates server membership from guest-room access so temporary participants do not receive visibility into the rest of your workspace.

Invite-scoped access

Guest sessions are connected to the specific invitation and room.

Role-aware membership

Owners, administrators, members, and guests have clearly different responsibilities.

No secrets in the interface

Sensitive values remain on the backend, not exposed in the Angular client.

Session boundaries

Temporary guest access can expire without affecting registered member accounts.
